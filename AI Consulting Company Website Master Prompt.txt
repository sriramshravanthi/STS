B.4 — DESIGN DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Direction:  "Technical document, premium print." Enterprise-credible,
              editorial, numerate. Deliberately NOT the default AI-startup look.
  Ground:     Warm bone paper (#F4F2EC) light / deep graphite (#0E1114) dark
  Ink:        #0F1216
  Accent 1:   Deep pine green #1C5D4A  (primary accent, used sparingly)
  Accent 2:   Burnt orange #C0521B     (single-use signal accent)
  Display:    Newsreader (editorial serif, variable)
  Body/UI:    Instrument Sans (grotesk with character - NOT Inter/Roboto)
  Mono:       IBM Plex Mono (section numbers, labels, metrics)
  Devices:    Numbered sections, hairline rules, sharp corners, asymmetric
              splits, generous vertical rhythm, subtle paper grain.
  Banned:     Purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, glowing orbs, neural-net
              hero art, robot imagery, stock photos, neon-on-black.
  Motion:     One staggered page-load reveal + IntersectionObserver scroll
              reveals. Fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  Themes:     Light + dark, via prefers-color-scheme and a persistent toggle.
  A11y:       WCAG 2.2 AA target, semantic landmarks, visible focus rings,
              full keyboard operation, no horizontal page scroll.
