## 2. What everyone does the same way

Across all 18 sites, the same eight devices appear almost without variation:

1. **Analyst-badge wall.** Gartner, Forrester Wave, IDC MarketScape, Everest PEAK, HFS Horizons. KPMG,
   Quantiphi, Cognizant, IBM and Fractal all lead with these.
2. **Partner logo grid.** AWS, Azure, GCP, Databricks, Snowflake, NVIDIA, OpenAI. Cognizant claims 85
   alliances; Accenture and Thoughtworks display near-identical grids.
3. **Round-number proof.** "75K trained consultants" (IBM), "nearly 3000 experts / 82+ patents" (BCG X),
   "6,000+ Fractalites", "50+ AI projects" (Markovate), "35,000+ clients" (Toptal).
4. **Three-to-five service pillars** as expandable cards. Universal.
5. **Client-logo carousel plus a named executive testimonial.**
6. **A gated report or newsletter** as the top-of-funnel asset.
7. **Industry vertical navigation** — 7 to 15 sectors, each with its own page.
8. **A single repeated CTA:** "Contact us" / "Get in touch" / "Book a discovery call."

**Consequence:** these pages are close to interchangeable. A buyer comparing five of them cannot
differentiate on anything except brand size.

## 3. What the best ones do that most don't

| Pattern | Who does it well | Why it works |

| **Sharp, verb-led positioning** | Tribe AI: *"AI that ships, scales, and compounds. We embed. We build. We don't stop until it's in production."* Thoughtworks: *"We don't just do AI / We do AI that works."* | States a position competitors could disagree with — the definition of real positioning |

| **Interactive maturity assessment** | Andela and Quantiphi both run one | The single most common high-intent lead magnet in this market — no email wall, instant value |
| **Named accelerators/products** | IBM Consulting Advantage; Thoughtworks AI/works™; BCG X Product Library; Quantiphi baioniq/Dociphi | Converts "we have experience" into something inspectable |
| **Pilot-first, low-risk entry** | Markovate (4–6 week scoped pilot); LeewayHertz (two-week validation sprints) | Removes the biggest objection — commitment before proof |
| **Explicit risk reversal** | Toptal (trial basis, "98% trial-to-hire", pay only if satisfied) | Directly attacks buyer risk rather than asserting quality |

## 4. The genuine gaps nobody fills

| Gap | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Nobody publishes pricing** | Zero of the 18 sites show a rate or range. Every path leads to a discovery call before a buyer can tell if the conversation is worth having. |
| **Nobody states limits** | Every site claims capability breadth. None states what it does not do or cannot yet claim. |
| **Consulting + build + training + assessment + talent is never integrated** | Big Four have the parts across separate P&Ls with separate account teams. Andela is closest (build + deploy + upskill) but has no strategy consulting layer. Tribe AI builds but does not train or staff. Toptal/Turing supply people but do not consult or build. |
| **Business-facing maturity tools are rare** | Only 2 of 18 offer one, despite it being the strongest lead magnet available. |
| **Skills assessment as a public tool is essentially absent** | Talent firms assert vetting rigour; none lets a visitor experience it. |

## 6. Resulting positioning

Everyone else says a version of *"we are experienced and recognised, book a call."*

NorthKey says: **here is the method, here is what it costs, here is a free tool that gives you a real answer
in two minutes, and here is a list of the things we are not.**

That is only available to a firm with no badge wall to defend — which makes the concept-stage weakness into
the position. It is also fragile in one specific way: **it must stay true as the company grows.** The moment
NorthKey earns SOC 2 or a real case study, the "what we don't claim" section must be updated honestly rather
than quietly deleted.

## 10. The AI Briefing column

Not one of the ~50 firms reviewed publishes anything on its site that updates daily. Insight pages are
refreshed monthly at best, and most carousels rotate the same items indefinitely. For a firm whose entire
pitch is *"we know what is actually happening in enterprise AI"*, a visibly current column is a
differentiator that costs the competition an editorial process they have not built.

**Architecture — three tiers, degrading honestly:**

```
Tier A  NORTHKEY_NEWS_ENDPOINT   A real feed the company hosts.
        window.NORTHKEY_NEWS_ENDPOINT = 'https://your-domain/api/briefing'
        Expected JSON:
          { "generatedAt": "2026-09-10T06:00:00Z",
            "items": [ {topic, title, detail, action, url, source} ] }
        Wins whenever it is set. A published Artifact cannot reach it —
        the sandbox blocks all external fetch — so this tier serves the
        self-hosted site.

Tier B  Artifact runtime          db + sample capabilities.
        A shared db document (briefing/current) caches ONE briefing per
        day. The first visitor of the day generates it; everyone else
        reads the cache. One generation per day, not one per visitor.

Tier C  Neither available         An honest empty state that explains
        why, with NO placeholder headlines. Filler dressed as current
        information is precisely the claim this firm says it won't make.
```

**Honesty constraints built into the generation prompt.** The model has no live feed, so it is explicitly
instructed not to invent news events, dates, funding rounds, product launches, announcements, benchmark
results, or sourced statistics. It writes about durable patterns and decisions. The column is labelled
**perspective, not reporting**, carries its generation date, and tells the reader to verify anything they
intend to act on. Presenting model output as verified news would be the exact failure mode the
"What we don't claim" section exists to prevent.

**To make it real news on the hosted site:** stand up a small scheduled function (Cloudflare Worker, Vercel
cron, or Lambda) that pulls curated RSS/API sources once a day, normalises them to the JSON shape above, and
serves it with permissive CORS. Then set `window.NORTHKEY_NEWS_ENDPOINT`. Tier A takes over automatically and
the `url`/`source` fields render as attributed outbound links.
