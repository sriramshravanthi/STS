AI Assessments

Ten tracks — AI, Generative AI, Python, Machine Learning, LLMs, Prompt Engineering, AI Engineering, MLOps, AI Agents, AI Architecture — each at beginner, intermediate, and advanced level. Every result names your strengths, names your gaps, and tells you what to do next.

Every result report contains

  • 01ScorePercentage and raw, broken down by section
  • 02Skill levelFoundational · Developing · Proficient · Advanced · Expert
  • 03StrengthsThe domains you scored above threshold, named
  • 04Skill gapsThe domains you scored below threshold, named specifically
  • 05Recommended coursesRanked Academy courses that close those exact gaps
  • 06Career pathThe role this profile fits now, and the next rung

8 questions · ~3 min
