Engineering standards

  • —Everything lives in your repository, under your license, from day one.
  • —Infrastructure as code. No consultant-configured console clicks.
  • —Evaluation suites ship with the feature and run in CI on every change.

Accelerators

Every serious AI firm has a library of reusable internal components. Ours exist to cut the boring 40% of a build — the scaffolding, the plumbing, the evaluation harness — so the budget goes into the part that is specific to you.

  • RetrievalGrounding KitChunking, embedding, re-ranking, citation enforcement, and a retrieval evaluation harness. Turns "we want to search our documents" into a measurable baseline in days rather than weeks.
  • AgentsAgent FrameTool registry, permission scoping, human approval gates, step-level tracing, cost and latency budgets, and a rollback path. The guardrails are the starting point, not the retrofit.
  • QualityEval BenchGolden datasets, automated evaluations in CI, regression gates on every prompt and model change, and drift monitoring. The component most teams skip and then wish they had.
  • DocumentsDoc PipelineClassification, extraction, validation, and confidence-based routing — straight-through where the model is sure, escalated to a person where it is not.
  • GovernanceControl PackModel inventory, risk tiering, review-board templates, audit logging standards, and an acceptable-use policy your employees can actually follow.
  • PlatformGround WorksInfrastructure-as-code baselines for AWS, Azure, and GCP — identity, networking, secrets, observability, and CI — so nothing is configured by hand in a console.
How this works commercially. Accelerators are not products we license to you and they carry no separate fee. They are delivered into your repository under your license along with everything else, and you are free to modify or discard them. We name them because "we have reusable components" is a claim; naming them is an invitation to ask what is in them.
