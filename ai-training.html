AI Training & Academy

Beginner to advanced, for individuals worldwide and for enterprise teams. When we have delivered a system for a client, the corporate curriculum is built on that system — not a generic syllabus.

  • AI FundamentalsHow AI actually works, what it can and cannot do, and where it belongs in a business.
  • Generative AI EssentialsModels, tokens, context, cost, and the practical limits of generation.
  • Prompt Engineering FoundationsStructure, examples, constraints, and evaluating your own prompts.
  • Python for AIThe subset of Python that AI work actually requires, taught fast.
  • AI for Business LeadersWhat to fund, what to refuse, and how to read an AI proposal.
  • Responsible AI BasicsBias, privacy, transparency, and where human review is non-negotiable.
  • Machine LearningSupervised and unsupervised methods, features, evaluation, and honest baselines.
  • Deep LearningNeural networks, training dynamics, transfer learning, and when not to use them.
  • Natural Language ProcessingExtraction, classification, summarization, and evaluation of text systems.
  • Working with LLMsStructured output, function calling, context management, and cost control.
  • Retrieval-Augmented GenerationChunking, embeddings, retrieval quality, citations, and permission-awareness.
  • AI AutomationDesigning document and workflow automation that survives real-world inputs.
  • Computer VisionDetection, classification, OCR pipelines, and production constraints.
  • AI Agents & Agentic SystemsTool use, orchestration, guardrails, blast radius, and human gates.
  • AI EngineeringBuilding production LLM systems: evaluation, observability, and reliability.
  • MLOps & Production MLDeployment, monitoring, drift, retraining, and CI for models.
  • AI ArchitectureEnterprise reference architecture, integration, and build-vs-buy at scale.
  • AI Security & Red-TeamingPrompt injection, data exfiltration, model abuse, and defensive design.
  • Enterprise AI Program LeadershipPortfolio, governance, funding, and organizational change.
  • Self-paced onlineVideo, labs, graded projects
  • Live instructor-ledCohort-based, scheduled, recorded
  • Workshops1–2 days, hands-on, team-level
  • Bootcamps8–12 weeks, portfolio, placement track
  • Corporate trainingDelivered on your own stack
  • Custom enterpriseBuilt on the system we delivered
Course catalog is in development. Certificates issued are certificates of completion. We do not describe them as certifications, because a certification implies an independently validated standard behind it.
