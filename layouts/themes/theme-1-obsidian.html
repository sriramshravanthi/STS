The method

Eight phases. Every one has an exit.

At the end of each phase you can stop, and the reason to stop is written down before it starts.

Phase 01

Frame

The problem in your words, written until both sides recognise it.

Phase 02

Assess

Whether your data and systems can actually carry the thing.

Phase 03

Design

Architecture, cost envelope and the failure modes named up front.

Phase 04

Prove

A narrow slice in production conditions, measured against a set you agreed.

Phase 05

Build

Your repo, your cloud, tested against the evaluation suite from phase four.

Phase 06

Deploy

Live, monitored, and reversible in a way somebody has actually rehearsed.

Phase 07

Operate

Drift, cost and quality watched by people who can act on what they see.

Phase 08

Scale

The second and third use case, cheaper than the first because of what phase five left behind.
