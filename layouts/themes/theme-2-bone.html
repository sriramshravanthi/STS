The NorthKey Method

Eight phases, each with a written exit right.

At the end of every phase you can stop — and the reason you might want to is agreed before the phase begins, not argued about afterwards.

Phase 01

Frame

Your problem written in your words, revised until both sides recognise it. Most engagements change shape here, and that is the cheapest place for it to happen.

Phase 02

Assess

Whether your data, systems and people can actually carry this. If the answer is no, you hear it now rather than after you have paid for a build.

Phase 03

Design

Architecture, cost envelope, and the failure modes named out loud while they are still cheap to design around.

Phase 04

Prove

One narrow slice under production conditions, measured against an evaluation set you agreed to in advance.

Phase 05

Build

In your repository and your cloud, tested continuously against the suite phase four produced.

Phase 06

Deploy

Live, monitored, and reversible by a rollback somebody has actually rehearsed.

Phase 07

Operate

Drift, cost and quality watched by people with the authority to act on what they see.

Phase 08

Scale

The second and third use case, cheaper than the first because of what phase five deliberately left behind.
