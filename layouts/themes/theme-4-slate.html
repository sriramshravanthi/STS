AI Briefing

What we’re watching.

Editorial

Evaluation

A pilot that passes tells you almost nothing about production

Demo conditions hide the cases that actually break a system: the messy document, the ambiguous query, the user who phrases things nobody anticipated.

Build the evaluation set before the build.

Unit economics

Inference cost moves from R&D budget to cost of goods

In a pilot the model spend is a rounding error. At scale it is a per-transaction cost sitting directly against gross margin, and nobody modelled it.

Price the cost per completed task, not per token.

Data readiness

Most AI programmes are blocked by retrieval, not by the model

Teams swap the model looking for a quality jump and meet the same failures, because the system never surfaced the right passage to begin with.

Measure retrieval quality on its own first.

Governance

Policy that is not a test does not gate anything

Principles get agreed in a steering meeting and then have no mechanism to stop a release. Governance binds only when it fails a build.

Turn each policy line into a pipeline check.
