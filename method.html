The NorthKey Method

Three beats, eight phases, and a written exit criterion at every one. You can stop at the end of any phase and keep everything produced up to that point — which is the opposite of how most AI engagements are sold.

  • Beat oneProveEstablish that the problem is real, the data supports it, and the money is there. Two phases, and a genuine right to walk away at the end of them.
  • Beat twoProduceDesign, build, and deploy the working system — in your repository, on your cloud, with evaluations running in CI from the first commit.
  • Beat threePass onTrain your people on this system, support it while they take it over, then scale to the next use case. Success is you not needing us.
01

Discover

Understand the client's business problem, the operators who live with it, and the money attached to it.

EXIT — A written problem statement both sides sign.
02

Assess

Analyze the data, technology, requirements, risks, and feasibility.

EXIT — A go/no-go with the reasoning shown.
03

Design

Create the AI solution, the architecture, the governance model, and the evaluation plan.

EXIT — Approved architecture and success metric.
04

Build

Develop and test the AI solution — in your repository, on your cloud, from the first commit.

EXIT — Working system passing its evaluation suite.
05

Deploy

Implement the solution in the client's environment, with monitoring and a proven rollback.

EXIT — Live, monitored, reversible.
06

Train

Train the client's employees on this system — their data, their workflows, their guardrails.

EXIT — Your staff operating it unaided.
07

Support

Ongoing maintenance, monitoring, model upgrades, and cost tuning.

EXIT — Agreed service levels being met.
08

Scale

Improve and expand the AI solution as the business grows, and take the next use case.

EXIT — Next phase scoped from measured results.

If the assessment says AI is not the right answer, we say so and stop. That is a legitimate outcome of Phase 02 — and a far cheaper one than the alternative.
