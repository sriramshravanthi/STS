How we price

  • 01Fixed fee per phaseNot hourly. You approve a scope and a price before work starts.
  • 02Phase-gatedExit at any gate and keep everything produced to that point.
  • 03No margin on softwareYou pay cloud, license, and model vendors directly. We take nothing.
  • 04Change orders priced firstOnly for scope you add, and always quoted before work begins.
Read these as planning ranges, not quotes. They exist so you can tell whether a conversation is worth having before you spend an hour on a call. Real pricing depends on scope, data condition, integration surface, and compliance requirements, and is fixed in writing in a proposal. A concept-stage firm should be honest that these are modelled figures rather than a rate card proven across many engagements — validate them against your own market before treating them as settled.
